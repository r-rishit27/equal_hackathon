Payables

Drafts become bill records. Workers check duplicates and enrich coding. Approval and disbursement are separate actions, with status and payment events recorded.

Evidence and variances

The reconciliation engine links an invoice to supporting evidence and compares billed lines. Finance can inspect discrepancies at line level and rerun a case when evidence changes.

Receivables

Bank credits and remittance are matched to open invoices. Policy governs eligible auto-application. Ambiguous matches go to review, with allocation history available for reversals.

Tenant controls

Service entitlements determine which modules are available. Role and capability checks govern actions within each module.

Provider boundary

OCR, vendor verification, banking and Nova usage depend on configured providers. Nova also has a separate reconciliation API.
