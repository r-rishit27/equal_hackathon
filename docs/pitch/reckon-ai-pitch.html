Every bill gets paid.
Almost none get checked.

A bill is a claim, not a fact. Until something compares it with what was ordered, what arrived and what was agreed, paying it is an act of trust — and a mid-size Indian finance team does that several hundred times a month.

Reckon.ai is the second pair of eyes on every rupee that leaves the company.

What one unchecked bill can cost
Cash

Paid twice. The duplicate leaves the account the same day — recovery is a favour, not a right.

Margin

Billed above the contract rate. A 4% slip on ₹50 lakh of annual spend is ₹2,00,000, and the ledger records it as correct.

Goods

Short delivery billed in full — you pay for what never arrived.

Compliance

Wrong TDS section: interest, a revised return and a notice, months later.

Illustrative arithmetic on ₹50 lakh of annual spend.
01 · The ledger records. It cannot check.Tally and Busy write down a wrong amount as faithfully as a right one. Ask either what the bill should have been and there is no answer — the PO, the goods receipt and the contract were never inside it.
02 · So the control is one tired person.Three hundred bills a month, seven manual steps each, most of them at quarter-end. Attention is the only control in the system — and attention is the first thing that runs out.
03 · Attention does not scale. Agents do.Twelve agents read, match, verify tax and route every bill the same way at 2 a.m. as at 10 a.m. Nothing is skipped because it was late, large or from a vendor everyone trusts.
